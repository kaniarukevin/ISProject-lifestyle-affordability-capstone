How Tosha uses your data

Tosha · version of 2026-11-07

This is the privacy policy for the Tosha web app at tosha-ke.netlify.app.

Who runs this app

Tosha is a final-year project by Kevin Kaniaru, a Computer Science student at Strathmore University, Nairobi. It is a study prototype, not a bank or a licensed financial adviser, and its answers are guidance, not financial advice.

What we keep

Your email address and a scrambled form of your password (never the password itself); your household details and income; the spending you enter; and from M-PESA statements you upload, each transaction's date, amount, type and the name of the person or business as the statement shows it, so the app can remember how you sorted them.

What we never keep

Uploaded statement files and their passwords. A statement is read in memory, you review what was found, and only the transactions you confirm are saved. The file and password are discarded.

Why

Only to work out your budget, your financial health score, your spending forecast and whether a choice is affordable, for you. Your data is not sold, not shared with anyone and not used for advertising. The app's models were trained on made-up data, not on users' data.

Where it is stored

In a cloud database (Neon) and on a cloud server (Render), which may be outside Kenya. Connections are encrypted, and the database host encrypts stored data.

Who can see it

Only you, through your account. The administrator sees counts and partly hidden email addresses, not your transactions.

Your rights

You can see everything the app keeps about you in the app, correct any entry, and delete your account at any time under You. Deleting removes all your data immediately and permanently, which also withdraws this consent. For any question, contact the developer through Strathmore University.

How long

Until you delete your account. All accounts will be deleted when the project ends.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Tosha receives only your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and uses the email address to find or create your account. It never gains access to anything else in your Google account, and it cannot post, read or send anything on your behalf. If you already have a Tosha account with the same email address, signing in with Google uses that same account rather than creating a second one.
